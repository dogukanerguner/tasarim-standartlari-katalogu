İngilizce prompt · detaylı
Design a Victorian tea house and apothecary website in the spirit of 19th-century engraved labels and William Morris. Cream parchment (#f4ecd8), near-black ink (#1e1a17), antique gold (#b08d3c), Morris green (#2f4f3a), claret (#7a1f2b). Everything centered. Hero framed by a 3px double border with a 1px inner rule and gold quarter-round corner flourishes; a double-ring medallion with ✦ inside, an "Established 1871" line with ❦ ❧ fleurons, a 78px Playfair Display 900 title, a Cinzel Decorative letterspaced subtitle, italic lead and double-bordered buttons. Product cards read as bottle labels: ivory ground, double rule, centered serif text, engraved placeholder images of 2px repeating horizontal lines, gold hairlines flanking a serif price. Leaf-pattern green bands between sections; no sans-serif, no single flat rules.
