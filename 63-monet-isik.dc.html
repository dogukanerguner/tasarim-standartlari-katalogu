Tasarım promptu — 63 — Monet · Işık seçip kopyalayın
İngilizce prompt · detaylı
Design a mobile weather and morning-ritual app in Claude Monet's impressionist style. Show the same screen in three lights, like his haystack series: three 260x560 phone mockups side by side — dawn haze (#f6d8c3 to #c9b8d8), noon (#a8c8e8 to #f0dfa8), sunset (#f2a65e to #c96a8d). Identical layout in each: a huge Cormorant Garamond temperature numeral with glow, blurred cloud color blobs, a 24px+ radius ritual card, and a lily-pond bottom panel of small rounded color dabs. No outlines, no black strokes, no grey shadows — every edge is a color transition.
