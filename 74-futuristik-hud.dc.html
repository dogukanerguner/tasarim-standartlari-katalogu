İngilizce prompt · detaylı
Design a futuristic spacecraft flight-control HUD in the Syd Mead / Minority Report lineage. Deep navy stage (#070b16) with a faint 48px cyan grid and a soft radial glow, never pure black. A single luminous cyan (#5ef2ff) carries all information: 1px translucent panel borders held by two-corner L-brackets, concentric orbital rings (one dashed, two partial arcs slowly rotating, a glowing satellite dot), hairline crosshairs, and a 72px light-weight altitude readout with text glow. Side panels show energy bars, system status rows and a countdown; red (#ff5c6c) appears only on the one item needing attention. Exo 2 uppercase with wide tracking for titles, monospace for readouts; frosted panels at 4% white, no rounded corners, no gradients on UI elements.
