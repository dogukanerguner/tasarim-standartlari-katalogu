Sarı bir trompettir
Kandinsky'ye göre her renk bir tınıdır: sarı keskin bir trompet, mavi derin bir org. Arayüzde renk süsleme değil, ses bilgisidir.

Nokta, çizgi, düzlem
Kandinsky'nin 1926 tarihli ders kitabı, bugünkü arayüz ızgarasının atasıdır: nokta vurgu, çizgi yön, düzlem sahnedir.

İngilizce prompt · detaylı
Design a synesthetic music-visualizer web app as if painted by Kandinsky. Cream canvas (#f4efe6) with near-black ink (#1d1b1a) and accents #d4462e, #2660a4, #e8a13c, #3f7d3a. The main stage is a Composition VIII-style arrangement where every shape IS the interface: a large concentric bass circle in the corner, rotated black line bundles for strings, pulsing colored dots for note hits, a checkerboard strip and semicircles, each with tiny 9px monospace instrument labels. Below it a dark player bar with segmented colored progress; on the right a shape-to-instrument legend panel and a recording widget whose mic level is shown by growing circles. Work Sans, hard 2px borders, offset flat shadow, no gradients on the page chrome, no photos.
