İngilizce prompt · detaylı
Design a pure-minimalist product page for a single ceramic cup, in the spirit of Kenya Hara and John Pawson. Off-white background (#fbfbfa) with near-black text (#1a1a1a) and warm grey neutrals (#f1f0ec, #e6e4de) — no accent color at all. A two-column hero split by a hairline: left, a square neutral product plate with the object centered in generous negative space; right, a tiny uppercase monospace eyebrow, a 40px light-weight (300) headline of one word, a 15px light paragraph capped at 34ch, a price row separated by a hairline, and one outlined uppercase button. Below, three principle columns and a four-item grid separated only by 1px gaps. Inter at two weights, no shadows, no radii, no icons, no decoration.
