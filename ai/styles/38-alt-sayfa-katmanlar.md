# 38 — Alt Sayfa & Katman

Bağlamı koruyan yarı katman ile yıkıcı eylemi durduran modal arasındaki fark.

## Prompt (EN)

Design a mobile layering study page. Phone one shows a bottom sheet: the underlying map list is blurred and dimmed but still visible, and a 400px-tall sheet with a 26px top radius, a drag handle, a title row with a close button, filter chips, a price list and a sticky action row slides up from the bottom. Phone two shows a blocking destructive-confirmation modal centred over a dark scrim, with a red primary action and a neutral cancel. Side notes compare sheet, modal and full screen.

## Brief (TR)

Bağlamı koruyan yarı katman ile yıkıcı eylemi durduran modalı yan yana gösteren katman çalışması.
