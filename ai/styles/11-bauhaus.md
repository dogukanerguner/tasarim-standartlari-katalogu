# 11 — Bauhaus

Temel geometrik şekiller, ana renkler ve asimetrik ağır tipografi.

## Prompt (EN)

Design a design-school landing page in strict Bauhaus style. Warm off-white background (#efe9dd) with 2px black rules dividing everything. An 88px uppercase heavy headline stacked as three words, one in red. On the right, a 2x2 block of primary-color panels containing a white circle, a black triangle, a white square and a text tile. A four-cell principle strip with oversized numbers in blue, yellow, red and inverted black. Pure geometry, no imagery, no ornament.

## Brief (TR)

Daire-kare-üçgen, üç ana renk ve ağır grotesk tipografiyle kurulmuş form-işlev sayfası.
