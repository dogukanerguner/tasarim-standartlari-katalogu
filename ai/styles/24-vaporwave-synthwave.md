# 24 — Vaporwave / Synthwave

## Prompt (EN)

Design an album landing page in synthwave style. Purple-to-black vertical gradient sky with a striped gradient sun (amber to magenta to violet) cut by horizontal bands, and an animated perspective grid horizon in cyan at the bottom. A 96px Orbitron uppercase title with a pulsing magenta and cyan glow. Wide 0.5em-tracked subtitle. A bordered translucent tracklist panel, a cassette-loop status card, and a 'A E S T H E T I C' block with extreme letter spacing.

Izgara ufuk çizgisi, çizgili gradyan güneş ve neon parlamayla kurulmuş 80'ler albüm sayfası.
