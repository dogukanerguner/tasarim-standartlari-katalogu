# 64 — Van Gogh · Yıldızlı Gece

Girdaplı gökyüzü, impasto doku ve fırça vuruşu ilerleme çubuğuyla bir uyku uygulaması.

## Prompt (EN)

Design a mobile sleep-tracking app in Vincent van Gogh's Starry Night style. One 300x640 phone mockup on a dark swirling page (#0f1a38 with faint repeating-conic texture). Screen sky in deep blue #1a2a5e with nested rotated conic-gradient swirl rings in #4a6db5 and #7b9fd4, thick yellow stars #f7d154 with glowing halo rings, and a crescent moon in the corner. Center sleep score 87 in Fraunces 700 over an impasto patch of short slanted gradient stripes. Progress bar is 13 small skewed brush-dab divs, not a solid bar. Bottom panel: dark cypress silhouette #0f1a38 with an SVG wavy top edge holding a textured sleep-stage chart in layered blues, orange window-light accent #e8944a. Avoid flat fills, thin lines, cold greys and static feel.

## Brief (TR)

Girdaplı lacivert gökyüzü, sarı yıldız vurguları ve fırça darbesi ilerleme çubuğuyla Yıldızlı Gece tablosu gibi kurulmuş uyku takip uygulaması.
