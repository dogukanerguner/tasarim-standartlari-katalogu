# 67 — Hokusai · Büyük Dalga

Ukiyo-e katmanları: Prusya mavisi dalga bizzat veri göstergesi, kırmızı mühür imza olur.

## Prompt (EN)

Design a Hokusai ukiyo-e surf-report app page. Paper background #f2e8d5 with faint print texture, strict flat color layers, no gradients or shadows; every box carries a 2px ink (#2b2b2b) woodblock contour. Hero: a Great Wave built in SVG — Prussian blue (#1b4470) curling crest, foam claws as rows of scallop circles, a small snow-capped Fuji triangle (#8a9bb0) behind. The wave IS the height gauge: show two states, a small 1.2m swell and a towering 3.4m wave. A 300x640 phone mockup lists wind, tide, period in contoured label boxes with one vertical-rl label, plus a square red hanko seal (#b5442c). Headings in Zen Antique.
