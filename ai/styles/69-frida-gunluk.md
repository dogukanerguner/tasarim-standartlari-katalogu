# 69 — Frida · Günlük

Kobalt duvar, botanik çerçeveler ve ex-voto kalp kartları; duygular saklanmaz, boyanır.

## Prompt (EN)

Design a Frida Kahlo emotion-journal mobile app page. Cobalt Casa Azul wall #1f4bb5 as page background. A 300x640 phone shows a cream #f5ead8 journal page framed by CSS botanicals: green #2e7d4f elliptic leaves and layered circle flowers in fuchsia #d81b60 and orange #ef6c00. Today's emotion is one big honest italic Fraunces word ("Ağır"). Replace the mood scale with ex-voto retablo heart cards — CSS hearts with flame, crack and flower variants. Add a Caveat handwritten entry with a votive date strip, a bead-necklace divider of colored dots, and a zigzag kilim repeating-linear-gradient strip instead of a bottom nav.

## Brief (TR)

Frida'nın kobalt duvarını ve ex-voto kalplerini bir duygu günlüğüne çeviren folk-art mobil sayfa.
