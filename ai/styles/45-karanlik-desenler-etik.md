Altı yaygın sömürücü desen, dürüst karşılıkları ve ekip için kontrol sorusu.

## Prompt (EN)

Design a dark warning-style page cataloguing dark patterns. Deep maroon-black background (#1a0f0d) with a coral accent (#ff8a72) and an outlined 'this page is a warning' badge. A phone mockup contrasts a cancellation screen: a red-tinted card describing the manipulative version (confirmshaming, hidden button, six-step maze) directly above the honest version (clear end date, optional discount, one-tap confirm, equally weighted buttons). Beside it, a six-row table pairing each dark pattern with its honest counterpart, and a boxed ethics test question for the team.
