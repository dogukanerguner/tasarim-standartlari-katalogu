Tasarım promptu — 65 — Picasso · Kübist seçip kopyalayın
İngilizce prompt · detaylı
Design a web social profile app in a Picasso cubist style. Beige canvas #e8d8b8, earth ochre #c8a24a, brick #b5502e, blue-period #3e6b8c, near-black #1c1a17. The profile "portrait" is fractured into 5-6 flat clip-path polygons showing the same face from different angles at once: a frontal circle eye, a black profile nose triangle, offset mouth bars, all outlined with thick black divs. Cards are deliberately rotated (-2deg to 1.5deg) in an asymmetric grid with 3-4px black borders and hard offset box-shadows. Section headings in Archivo 800 uppercase sit on shifted color blocks like cut-and-paste collage; one card carries a newspaper texture strip of thin repeating lines. Avoid symmetry, photographic realism, soft gradients and single perspective.
Türkçe brief
Profil portresini clip-path poligonlara bölen, kartları döndürülmüş, kalın siyah konturlu ve kolaj dokulu kübist sosyal uygulama.
