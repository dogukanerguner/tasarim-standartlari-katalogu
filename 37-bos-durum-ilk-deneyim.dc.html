İngilizce prompt · detaylı
Design a mobile empty-state study page. Two phone mockups: the first shows a true empty state — a dashed 120px placeholder tile, a non-blaming headline, one sentence of guidance, one primary button, one secondary text link, and a sticky setup progress bar showing 2 of 5 complete. The second shows a half-finished checklist where two items are struck through with filled check circles, the next item is bold with a '30 sn' time estimate, and remaining items are greyed. Side cards explain the three jobs of an empty state and time-to-first-value.
