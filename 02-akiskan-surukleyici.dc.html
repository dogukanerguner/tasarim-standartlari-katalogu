İngilizce prompt · detaylı
Design an award-style creative agency landing page with a fluid, immersive feel. Near-black background (#0e0d12) with three huge blurred color orbs (electric blue, teal, violet) slowly drifting behind the content. Centered ultra-light display headline at 130px with one word in italic semibold. Frosted glass pill buttons with backdrop blur and thin light borders. A slow horizontal marquee strip of uppercase keywords. Full-bleed video placeholder with rounded 22px corners. Everything feels weighted, cinematic and continuously in motion.
