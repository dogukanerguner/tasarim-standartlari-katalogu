İngilizce prompt · detaylı
Design a bohemian, Anthropologie-style landing page for a fictional ceramics and weaving studio ("Toprak & Yün"). Warm beige ground (#efe3d0) with a faint 22px dot texture, a terracotta (#c96f4a) hero block on the right holding arch-topped image placeholders (border-radius 999px 999px 0 0) filled with soft vertical gradients and thin repeating-linear-gradient weave lines, a macramé fringe of uneven 2px vertical strands, olive (#6b7d4f) rotated-ellipse leaves, and a sun badge made of a circle with ray lines. Headline in Cormorant Garamond 78px medium with an italic olive word; handwritten Caveat accents rotated -3deg; pill-shaped terracotta button. Cards on sand (#e9d8c0) with 24px radius and dotted 1.5px borders; ink text #3f2f25. No sharp corners, no cool greys, no pure white.
