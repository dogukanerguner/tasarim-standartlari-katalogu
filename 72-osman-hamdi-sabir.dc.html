İngilizce prompt · detaylı
Design a focus-and-habit app landing born from Osman Hamdi's The Tortoise Trainer (1906). Dim plaster wall (#3b2415, #a8845a); all content sits inside a huge arch (border-radius 610px 610px 0 0) lit by a warm radial glow from the top, with ochre #c96f2e, Iznik #1f6f5c and robe-red #8a2f1f accents. Center a 300x640 phone on warm cream (#e8d5b5): the focus timer is a progress ring on which a tiny CSS tortoise crawls via a 90s rotation, plus a gentle no-urgency reminder card. Right column lists habits as baby tortoises whose shells grow richer at 3, 21 and 90 days. Spectral headings, monospace digits. No neon, no countdown anxiety, no gamification pressure.
