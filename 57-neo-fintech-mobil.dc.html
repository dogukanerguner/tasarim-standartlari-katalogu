İngilizce prompt · detaylı
Design a neo-fintech mobile banking home screen in the Monzo/Revolut school. Dark navy #0f1225, Inter 400-700, all amounts bold tabular numerals. Top: greeting, a notification dot, a tiny CSS bar sparkline. Center: a huge 34px balance (₺12.480,50), then a tilted hero bank card (200x126px, 14px radius, #6a3df5 to #b84ffb gradient, chip square, last four digits, fictional "NOVA" brand). Below: four quick-action circles (Send, Request, FX, Freeze) on #191d33 surfaces, then a "Today" list with emoji category circles (🛒 ☕ 🚕), white amounts for spending, green #2ecc71 "+" for income, one faded upcoming-payment row. No bank gray, no dense tables, no tiny numbers.
