Brütalist & ProvokatifStefan Sagmeister ekolü
Tasarım promptu — 04 — Brütalist & Provokatif seçip kopyalayın
İngilizce prompt · detaylı
Design a provocative anti-design poster page for a design studio. Warm paper background (#f2e8d5) with a visible halftone dot texture. Massive 130px Archivo Black uppercase headline where each line is rotated a few degrees, one word knocked out in a red block, one word set in outline-only text stroke. A yellow circular sticker rotated and wobbling that says the button does nothing. Struck-through nav items in italic. Hard 3px black borders, a black inverted column, deliberately mismatched baselines.
Türkçe brief
Kuralları kasten bozan, döndürülmüş tipografi ve ironik mikro metinlerle kurulmuş provokatif afiş sayfası.
