Tasarım promptu — 08 — Bento Grid seçip kopyalayın
İngilizce prompt · detaylı
Design a consumer hardware product page built entirely from a bento grid. Light grey background (#f1f1f3), white cards with 26px radii and a barely-there shadow. A 4-column grid with 186px rows: one 2x2 hero cell with a 52px headline, one wide black stat cell with a huge 2.8x number, one gradient color-engine cell, one battery cell with three progress bars, and small cells for privacy and price. Each cell holds exactly one idea, generous internal padding, no card is decorative.
